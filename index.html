02

the re-order rules.

One rule for every part wastes either cash or time. The class and the lead time decide the rule: expensive parts follow the build plan, catalogue parts get a reorder point, and cheap parts live in two bins on the line. Change the build plan above and watch the status column.

From the job adTimely re-ordering and pro-active re-order rules, based on production planning and consumption data.
Order to plan

A parts and made to drawing

Demand comes from the build plan, not from history. POs go out one lead time before the build, as monthly call-offs on a frame agreement.

Reorder point

Catalogue and long-lead parts

Order when stock plus open orders drops below lead time demand plus safety stock. Safety stock grows with the service level and the lead time.

Two bins / VMI

Cheap, fast moving parts

Two bins at the line, refill one when it empties, or the supplier refills them every week. Zero planning time for parts worth about 4% of the spend.

Method

need/wk = plan x qty x attach rate x (1 + spares) / 4.33

safety stock = z x 0.35 x need/wk x √lead time, with z = 1.28, 1.65, 2.05 for 90, 95, 98%.

reorder point = need/wk x lead time + safety stock

suggested PO = need over lead time + review period + safety stock, minus stock and open POs, rounded up to the MOQ

cost per robot = unit cost + tooling / (3 x yearly volume)
